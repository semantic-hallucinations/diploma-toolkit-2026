## Important Tension

`СТП 01-2024` refers to `ГОСТ 7.1-2003` for the list of used sources. At the
same time, public catalog/status sources show `СТБ 7.1-2024` as the active
Belarusian bibliographic-description standard. Treat this as a question for
normal control: the safest route is to follow the department's current
instructions or BSUIR's current methodical recommendations for source lists.

## Other Standards Mentioned By `СТП 01-2024`

Depending on the content of the diploma project, `СТП 01-2024` also references
other ESKD/ESTD/ESPD standards, including standards for:

- computer-output documentation;
- kinds and completeness of design documents;
- main inscriptions;
- educational technical posters;
- technical drawings and assembly drawings;
- process documentation;
- physical units and symbols;
- electrical schematic symbols and line types.

For an AI/software diploma, the most immediately relevant are usually:

- `ГОСТ 19.701-90` for algorithm/program/data/system schemes;
- `ГОСТ 2.701-2008` for scheme classification;
- `ГОСТ 2.105-95` and `ГОСТ 2.106-96` for the text document baseline;
- `СТП 01-2024` itself for BSUIR-specific layout, structure, title page,
  graphical material, references, and defense documentation.
